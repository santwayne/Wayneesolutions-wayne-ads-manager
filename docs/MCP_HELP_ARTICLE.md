# Use Wayne Ads Manager in Claude

Connect Claude to Wayne Ads Manager to check how your Facebook and Instagram ads are doing and make safe changes by just asking.

## Connect
1. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors**, find **Wayne Ads Manager** and click **Connect**.
2. Sign in with your Wayne Ads Manager account and click **Allow**.
3. You only see the businesses your account has access to. Viewers can read; Managers and Owners can make changes.

## What you can ask
- "How did my ads do this week?" for spend, leads, cost per lead, and customers won from your CRM
- "Show me the alerts" for ads burning money, expensive ads, winners, tired creatives, rejected ads
- "What should I do this week?" for the 3 recommended actions from the Monday report
- "Draft a WhatsApp campaign for my cafe, ₹400 a day, 5 km around Sarabha Nagar". This creates a draft only. Add photos in the app, then launch.
- "Pause the Canada visa ad" or "Increase the demo class budget by 20%". Claude asks you to confirm first.

## Safety
- New campaigns are always created paused. Nothing goes live without a person approving it.
- Budget increases are limited to 20% per change and never exceed your daily cap.
- Every change is in **Kya badla (activity log)** in the app, and the owner can undo the last change in one click.
- Billing and payment methods are never touched.

## Disconnect
Remove the connector in Claude's settings. Access tokens stop working immediately.
